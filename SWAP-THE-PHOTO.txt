AFRIKANADOLLZ — how to change photos
=====================================

All images live in the /assets folder. To swap one, just replace the file
with a new one of the SAME name (keep the filename identical), then redeploy.

  assets/hero.jpg    → the big hero model + "Ready-to-Ship" tile + first product card
  assets/dolls.jpg   → "Bundles & Closures" tile + second product card
  assets/blonde.jpg  → "American Blues" tile + fourth product card
  assets/curly.jpg   → the Atelier close-up + third product card
  assets/squad.jpg   → the "We're Afrikanadolls" story photo
  assets/logo.png    → the crest logo (header, story seal, footer)

Best results:
  - hero.jpg     portrait, ~900 x 1300 px
  - dolls/squad  landscape or group, ~1100 px wide
  - curly/blonde portrait close-up, ~640 x 870 px
  - Export as JPG, quality ~80, keep each under ~250 KB for fast loading.

That's it. Drop the file in, redeploy, done.
