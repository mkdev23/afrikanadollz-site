Deposit

A $20 deposit is required to secure every appointment, paid via Cash App $Afrikanadolls when you book. It's applied toward your total service cost — the deposit itself is never an extra charge.

Location

Our studio is at 281 S 52nd St, Philadelphia, PA 19139. Full entry and parking details are included in your appointment confirmation email once your deposit is received.

Guests

We keep the studio calm and on schedule — please don't bring extra guests. Space for anyone beyond the client being serviced is limited and can't be promised.

Grace period

We hold your slot for 20 minutes past your scheduled start time. Arriving later than that may mean a shortened service, a reschedule, or forfeiting your deposit, depending on the day's schedule.

Same-day & squeeze-in

Booking the same day, or asking to be fit into an already-scheduled day, carries an additional $40 fee on top of your service price and deposit.

Remaining balance

Your remaining balance (service total minus your deposit) is due the day of your appointment, paid by Zelle or cash.

Plans change — here's how it works

Cancellations, rescheduling & no-shows.

We hold your exact time slot for you alone, which means a late cancellation or a no-show directly costs us that appointment slot — these terms exist to keep the schedule fair for everyone.

Cancelling 48+ hrs ahead

Cancel at least 48 hours before your appointment and you'll be refunded 100% of your deposit.

Cancelling <48 hrs ahead

Cancellations made less than 48 hours before your appointment are not refunded — no exceptions.

Rescheduling

Need a different time instead of cancelling outright? Text us at least 48 hours ahead at (267) 481-4058 and we'll do our best to get you a new slot — the same cancellation window above still applies to any change made with less notice.

No call / no show

If you can't make it, let us know at least 24 hours ahead by call or text. A no-call/no-show forfeits your deposit and means we're unable to book you again going forward.

Come prepared

Sew-ins, installation & braiding prep.

A few service-specific requirements that help your appointment run on time and your unit or style come out right.

Sew-ins & installation

If you're supplying your own previously-used hair, it must arrive washed and in good, wearable condition. We can't work with overly-cut bundles or wefts, or hair with leftover old thread still attached — bundles that don't meet this standard may be declined at your appointment. Installs using more than 3 bundles carry an additional $20 fee.

Braiding

Come with your natural hair washed, blow-dried, and fully detangled. Listed braiding prices are for labor only and don't include hair — braiding hair can be purchased through the studio at $5 per pack if you need it.

Processing time

Every order is packed by hand by our small studio team, not a warehouse — please allow 2–5 business days for your order to be processed before it ships. You'll get a shipping confirmation email once it's on its way.

Shipping area

We currently ship within the United States only. Shipping cost and estimated delivery window are calculated at checkout based on your address and the weight of your order.

Tracking

A tracking number is emailed to you as soon as your order ships. Delivery estimates come from the carrier and aren't guaranteed by us.

Lost, delayed or stolen packages

Once a carrier marks a package "delivered," we're not able to take responsibility for it being lost, stolen, or misdelivered afterward — please contact the carrier directly, and we'll help you file a claim where we can. We're also not responsible for carrier delays outside our control.

Incorrect address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We aren't able to cover reshipping costs for orders sent to an address that was entered incorrectly.

Order changes

Need to change or cancel an order? Contact us right away — we can only make changes before an order has shipped.

Returns & exchanges.

Because these are hair products, our return policy follows standard hygiene practice across the hair and beauty industry.

Final sale

Once a unit has been worn, installed, cut, colored, styled, or removed from its original packaging, it's final sale and can't be returned or exchanged. This protects every client who purchases from us afterward.

Unworn / defective items

Unworn units still in their original condition and packaging may be eligible for an exchange or store credit within 3 business days of delivery if there's a genuine manufacturing defect. Contact us with photos or video of the issue first — items sent back without prior approval won't be accepted.

Refund method

Eligible returns are issued as store credit or an exchange — not a cash/card refund.

Custom units

Fully custom wigs made to your own specifications are final sale and can't be returned or exchanged, since they can't be resold to another client.

Your information

Privacy policy.

A plain-language explanation of what we collect, why, and who (if anyone) it's shared with.

Information we collect

  • Contact & booking info — name, phone number, email address, appointment notes, and (optionally) an inspiration photo you choose to upload when booking.
  • Account info — if you create an account, we keep your email/phone, name, and appointment/billing history. We sign you in with a one-time emailed link or texted code — we never ask you to create or store a password for your customer account.
  • Hair profile info — hair type, texture, allergy notes, and styling preferences you optionally share with us to make future visits smoother. Our stylist may also keep her own private working notes about your appointments for her own reference — those notes are never shown back to you.
  • Billing records — deposits and balances are handled by hand via Cash App, Zelle, or cash. We keep a record of what was paid and when, but we never process, transmit, or store your card or bank details ourselves.
  • Retail orders — if you buy retail hair or wig products, your order and checkout (including payment) is handled directly by our e-commerce provider, Shopify, under its own privacy policy. We don't receive or store your full payment details.

The virtual try-on mirror

Our Try-On Mirror runs entirely inside your own browser. Your camera feed, and any photo you use there, is never uploaded or sent anywhere — unless you separately and explicitly opt in to "Diaka Style Suggestions," which sends that one photo to our AI image-processing provider for analysis and isn't stored afterward.

How we use your information

To schedule and manage appointments, send confirmations and (if you opt in) text reminders, maintain your account and order history, answer your questions, and generally run the studio. We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the providers that help us run the studio and this site: our cloud hosting, database, and communications provider (Microsoft Azure — including Azure Communication Services for email/SMS, and Azure OpenAI only for the opt-in style-suggestion feature), and Shopify for retail purchases. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone else.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of essential cookies to keep you signed in to your customer account, or to the studio's private admin tools. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Text messages

If you opt in to appointment reminder texts, message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

How long we keep it

We keep appointment, billing, and account records for as long as reasonably needed for scheduling history and recordkeeping (including tax purposes). Inspiration photos are kept as part of the related appointment record.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us at (267) 481-4058 or admin@afrikanadollz.com. We'll honor reasonable requests, though we may need to keep certain records as required by law.

Children

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — the version posted on this page is always the current one.
